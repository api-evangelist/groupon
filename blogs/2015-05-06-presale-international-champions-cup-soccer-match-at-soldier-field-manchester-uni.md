International Champions Cup In an effort to crank up the competition of the European club preseason tour, the International Champions Cup kicked off in 2013 as an eight-team competition spread across six U.S. cities. The tournament doubled in size in 2014, highlighted by a record-breaking match between Real Madrid and Manchester United that drew more than 109,000 fans to the University of Michigan’s “Big House.” Like a bubble borne from a million pieces of gum, the ICC will expand across the globe this summer with editions in China and Australia. On North American soil, the tournament will unfold in 11 cities at esteemed venues , including a stop at Soldier Field in Chicago. There, Wayne Rooney, Robin van Persie, and the rest of Manchester United ’s star-filled roster will square off against an equally talented Paris Saint-Germain squad.
